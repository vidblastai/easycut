Why minutes, and not videos?

Almost everything a video costs us scales with how much footage went in, not with how many clips came out. A plan sold as “three videos” would have to either refuse your hour-long podcast or lose money on it — so the meter is minutes, and the number of videos is what that buys. The counts are deliberately cautious: ten minutes of footage per short, twenty per long-form. Film tighter than that and you get more.

Why is my footage deleted before my video?

Your original file is roughly twenty times the size of the finished video, and once the edit exists the only thing it is needed for is re-cutting. So we hold it for the window your plan buys — long enough to change your mind — and keep the finished video far longer. After the footage goes you can still watch and download what you made; you just cannot re-render it.

Privacy

You upload footage of yourself talking. That is about as personal as a file gets, so this page says plainly what happens to it — what we keep, who else sees it, and when it is deleted.

What we collect

Your account
Email address, and your name if you gave one. Sign-in is handled by Clerk; we never see or store a password.
Your footage
The video file you upload, and the proxy and audio track we derive from it to do the work.
What you said
A transcript with word-level timings. It is the thing the whole edit is built from.
The edit itself
The finished video, its thumbnail, and the edit decision list, so you can change it later without paying to analyse the footage again.
How much you have used
Minutes of footage processed this month, against your plan’s allowance.
What we do not collect
No advertising identifiers, no cross-site tracking, no third-party analytics.

Who else sees it

Transcription
Your audio — not the video — goes to a speech-to-text provider to produce the transcript.
The AI director
The transcript text decides the hook, the cuts and where B-roll belongs. Your video is never sent — only the words.
B-roll and images
Short search terms derived from what you said. No part of your footage is sent.
Storage and payments
Files with Cloudflare R2 or Amazon S3; cards go to Stripe directly and never reach our servers.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your footage, transcripts or finished videos to train any model of our own.

How long we keep it

Two clocks, because the two things are different sizes and have different uses.

Deletion is automatic and permanent. Once your footage has gone you can still watch and download your finished video, but it can no longer be re-edited or exported in another shape, because re-rendering needs the original. Delete a project yourself and everything belonging to it goes immediately.

What you can ask for

A copy of anything you have made, deletion of any project or your whole account, correction of your name and email, and an answer to any question about where your data sits.

Terms

The short version: you keep your footage and everything we make from it, you pay monthly for a set amount of footage, and either of us can walk away at the end of a month.

What EasyCut does

You upload video of yourself talking. We transcribe it, decide where the cuts and the captions and the B-roll go, and render a finished video. You can change any of it in the editor while we still hold your footage.

The edit is made by software making judgement calls. It is usually good and it is sometimes wrong — that is what the editor is for. Watch a video before you post it.

Your content stays yours

You own your footage and you own the finished videos. You give us permission to do the one thing you are paying for: store your file, process it, and send the necessary parts to the services that do transcription and editing decisions. That permission ends when the content is deleted. We do not train models on your work.

You are responsible for having the right to upload what you upload, including anyone else who appears or is audible in it.

What you may not do

Nothing illegal, nothing you do not have the rights to, nothing made to harass or impersonate someone. Do not resell access to the service, and do not work around your plan’s allowance with multiple accounts.

Plans, payment and cancellation

Plans are monthly and charged in advance, and each includes an amount of footage measured by the length of the files you upload. When the allowance runs out, new videos wait until it resets on your billing date; nothing you have already made is affected. Unused footage does not roll over.

Cancel whenever you like and the plan runs to the end of the month you have paid for. Since a plan is consumed as you use it we do not refund part-months as a rule — but if the software genuinely failed you, write to us and we will sort it out.

How long we keep things

Two clocks, set by your plan: how long we keep the footage you uploaded, and how long we keep the finished videos. Deletion is automatic and permanent, and the full schedule is in the privacy policy. Download anything you want to keep.

What we do not promise

We work hard to keep this running and we do not promise it never breaks. Where something goes wrong on our side, what we owe you is limited to what you paid us in the previous month. Keep your own copy of anything that matters — we are a tool in your workflow, not your archive.
