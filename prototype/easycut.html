Everything a good editor would add.

Not filters on top of your video — an actual edit, built from what you said.

Captions

Word-perfect, animated, with the words that matter picked out in your accent colour.

B-roll

Real footage cut in wherever you name something concrete. Never generic, never over your punchline.

Motion graphics

Numbers become stat cards. Lists build in. Named ideas get an animated icon.

Sound design

Whooshes on cuts, pops on graphics, a music bed that ducks under your voice automatically.

Transitions

Whip pans, zoom punches and glitches — placed only on real cuts, never for decoration.

Reframing

Shot landscape, posting vertical? We track you through the frame so you never lose your head.

Pick a look.

Each one changes the pacing, the captions, the sound design and how much happens on screen. Switch after the edit and re-render for free.
